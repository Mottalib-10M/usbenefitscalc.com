SUBJECT: SNAP – Fiscal Year 2027 Cost-of-Living Adjustments 
TO: All State Agencies 
Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program 
This memorandum provides the fiscal year (FY) 2027 Cost-of-Living Adjustments 
(COLA) to the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) maximum allotments, 
income eligibility standards, and deductions. In accordance with the Food and Nutrition 
Act of 2008 (“the Act”), the COLAs are effective as of October 1, 2026. 
Attached are FY 2027 COLAs for the 48 contiguous States and the District of Columbia 
(D.C.), Alaska, Guam, Hawaii, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. 
The maximum allotments will increase for the 48 States and D.C., Alaska, Guam, and 
the U.S. Virgin Islands. The maximum allotment for a family of four in the 48 States and 
D.C., will be $1,023. Maximum allotments for a family of four will range from $1,306 to 
$2,027 in Alaska. The maximum allotment for a family of four will be $1,507 in Guam 
and $1,315 in the U.S. Virgin Islands. The maximum allotment for a family of four in 
Hawaii will decrease to $1,655. The minimum benefit for the 48 States and D.C. will 
increase to $25. 
Section 3(u)(2) of the Act outlines the process for adjusting SNAP maximum allotments 
by household size. This provision requires an additional 22 percent of the maximum 
four-person allotment per person for households of nine or more persons. It also caps 
the total benefits for those additional persons at 200 percent of the maximum four-
person allotment. In effect, for FY 2027, this caps the maximum allotment for 
households with 18 or more persons at $3,887 in the 48 States and the District of 
Columbia. The maximum allotment cap is different for Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, 
Alaska, and Hawaii. 
The shelter cap value will increase to $769 for the 48 States and D.C. The shelter cap 
values for Alaska, Guam, Hawaii, and the U.S. Virgin Islands will also increase. The
