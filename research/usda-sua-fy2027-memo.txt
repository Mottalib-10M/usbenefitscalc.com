SUBJECT: Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) – Simplified Process for 
Fiscal Year 2027 Standard Utility Allowance (SUA) Values  
TO:  All SNAP State Agencies 
  All Regions  
 
On August 15, 2025, the Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) published the memo titled 
“Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)- Simplified Process for Fiscal Year 2026 
Standard Utility Allowance (SUA) Values”, which provided State agencies with a simplified 
option for calculating their Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 SUA values. State agencies were encouraged 
to use this option while FNA reviewed SNAP regulations pertaining to SUAs and determined 
changes necessary to comply with the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025 (OBBB). 
For FY27, FNA is continuing to offer State agencies a simplified option as changes from OBBB 
continue to be implemented. State agencies may not use a new SUA methodology to 
calculate their SUA values without approval from FNA and are required to update their SUA 
values each year. Due to these requirements, FNA strongly urges State agencies to utilize the 
following approach. 
Simplified Process for Calculating FY27 SUA Values 
To determine FY27 SUA values, State agencies may adjust their FY26 SUA values by the 
change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) for All Items between June 2025 and June 20261. 
FNA will readily approve SUA values calculated using this method.  
State agencies can submit their proposed values using this adjustment method to their 
respective FNA Regional Office for expedited review and approval. If applicable, State 
agencies should include their approved standard medical deduction (SMD) offset value as 
part of their submissions. 
 
1 The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for All items (not seasonally adjusted) increased 3.5 percent between June 2025 
and June 2026. State agencies can find more information at: https://www.bls.gov/cpi/data.htm.
